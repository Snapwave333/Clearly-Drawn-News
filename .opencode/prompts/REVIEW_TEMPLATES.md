# Reviewer templates

## Research source review
- claim_id, asserted_fact, citation_url, source_date, source_kind, direct_support, contradictory_source, verdict PASS/REVISE/REJECT, severity, exact_revision.

## Script/retention hypothesis review
- scene_id, beat_id, educational_goal, viewer_question, attention-risk_hypothesis, potentially_confounded_by, suggested_edit, reference_to_prior_episode, experiment_needed, decision.

## Visual review
- timestamp/fps/frame, inspected rendered frame URL/path, factual/legibility/contrast/motion notes, original artwork check, pass/fail, remediation.

## Analytics review
- video_id, data_observation_window, metrics_requested, metrics_available, auth_status, sample_maturity, low-sample caution, retention moments, scene join, hypotheses not claims, next experiment.

## Optimizer release decision
- experiment_id, preregistered prediction, evidence, comparability, confounders, QA gate, originality gate, policy gate, config diff, rollout fraction, rollback pointer, audit approval.
