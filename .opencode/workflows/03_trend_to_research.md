# Trend scouting and research rigor

## Pipeline
1. **SIGNALS** — capture RSS / official popular video data / DuckDuckGo at observed timestamp
2. **PITCH** — develop specific teachable question and novelty analysis
3. **PRIMARY** — seek authoritative source, date and rights facts
4. **LEDGER** — split narrative into sourced claim IDs
5. **CHALLENGE** — fact-checker independently verify quantitative/current claims
6. **ACCEPT** — producer greenlights script only after research gate

## Rules
Never treat a public conversation as proof; developing stories should identify what is not known. Avoid generic "top facts" scripts.

## Exit evidence
A completed workflow emits immutable artifact hashes, review gate statuses, agent/event trace IDs, and clear failure records. Do not substitute a successful LLM response for a verified native asset, fetched metric or upload confirmation.
