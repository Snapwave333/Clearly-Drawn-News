# Experiment-led reversible improvement

## Pipeline
1. **OBSERVE** — collect mature authorized analytics, editorial notes and factual/craft quality outcome
2. **HYPOTHESIZE** — single variable change and explicit prediction
3. **PREREGISTER** — metric, time window, comparable cohort, minimum data and stop rules
4. **CANARY** — run a small cohort of future episodes with variant vs existing baseline
5. **AUDIT** — independent auditor checks novelty, factual accuracy and policy compliance
6. **DECIDE** — adopt, continue observation or reject based on evidence; avoid causal leaps
7. **ROLLBACK** — restore previous creative settings on regression and archive trial
8. **REPEAT** — small parameter evolution with topic diversity and artistic human review

## Rules
Feedback must NOT modify code, credentials, scope, disclosure, QA gates or upload permissions. Self-improvement is versioned creative-config evolution, not recursively self-authoring unreviewed policies.

## Exit evidence
A completed workflow emits immutable artifact hashes, review gate statuses, agent/event trace IDs, and clear failure records. Do not substitute a successful LLM response for a verified native asset, fetched metric or upload confirmation.
