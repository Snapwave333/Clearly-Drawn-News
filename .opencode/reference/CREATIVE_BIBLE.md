# The Living Whiteboard — art direction and anti-slop bible

## House voice
Make every explanation look like an expert illustrator planned it to teach, not a generic template engine. A disciplined visual system supports distinct per-episode creative metaphors.

## Shot taxonomy
1. **Ink trace:** a path is drawn in the order a human would actually use to explain it.
2. **Exploded mechanism:** linked parts separate while maintaining spatial and causal meaning.
3. **Reveal by contradiction:** draw the naive model, then visibly demonstrate where it fails.
4. **Frame-within-frame:** a small diagram grows into the system it represents.
5. **Dynamic comparison:** animate two alternatives on a shared truthful scale.
6. **Variable sweep:** move exactly one input parameter and show the corresponding output.
7. **Deconstruction:** peel one layer of a mechanism at a time, leaving physical context.
8. **Camera as teacher:** zoom/reframe only to reveal a relationship not visible at previous scale.
9. **Sketch-to-model bridge:** ink drawing morphs into Blender geometry for a necessary 3D insight.
10. **Scientific evidence board:** cite data with units and visual uncertainty rather than misrepresenting estimates as measurements.

## Art rules
- Visual subject precedes decoration. Non-informative movement, confetti, UI-card wipes, randomly changing gradients and stock icons do not belong.
- Use a consistent line-width system and compositional grammar; customize motifs for each episode.
- Favor 2–3 simultaneous concepts per visual, with extra detail revealed as narration needs it.
- Treat white space as a pacing tool. Avoid busy backgrounds that reduce comprehension.
- Check factual diagrams against source geometry and domain editor review.
- Originality constraints: compare composition similarity, reused beats, repetitive hooks, copied creator designs and repetitive template shots.
- Explain what is illustrative and what is measured, especially for scaled animations, historical events, experiments and simulations.
- Originality is an audited goal, not a guarantee that no viewer will ever identify AI involvement. Clearly credit AI-assisted production.

## Typography
Text serves labels, formulas and scientific citations. Never make the video a slideshow of narration transcripts. Keep words low and legible, with mobile tests at ~360-pixel-wide previews.

## Motion
Informational energy comes from explanation. Use pauses where cognition needs it; cuts should answer questions or move between levels of scale. Speeding narration to force a duration is forbidden.

## Editorial sound
Clean neutral Kokoro voice performance, restrained creative inflection, properly licensed subtle pen/fidget/diagram sounds. Avoid a constant elevator-music bed or meme effects.

## QA test
For any randomly sampled 10 seconds: What single insight did the scene convey? If the answer is merely "it looks animated", rewrite the visual plan.
